## Study summary
Five participants gave recorded think-aloud reviews comparing the original Punjab National Bank website with my modified Figma prototype. Each reviewed the overall experience rather than a single fixed task. Audio was converted to text and coded for recurring themes.

## Final conclusion (summary)
**On the qualitative evidence, the prototype improved the user experience.** All 5 participants preferred it.

- **Clutter** was raised by 5 of 5 participants on the original site; all 5 found the prototype simpler and better organised.
- **Login** was hard to find on the original (one participant reported about 2 minutes) and immediately visible on the prototype.
- **Loan, savings and account options** were hard to find on the original and visible on the prototype's homepage.
- **Chatbot** was unexpected or hard to find on the original, and clearly placed on the prototype.
- **Imagery and colour** were judged weaker on the original; the prototype's photography and red-yellow scheme were praised.
- **New features** valued: search with popular searches, a screen reader option, language options, and an AI-style message box.
